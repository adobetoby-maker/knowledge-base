# Link Building for Small Business Sites

## What Links Actually Do

Backlinks are votes of confidence. A link from a trusted website tells Google that your content is worth citing. The domain authority of the linking site matters more than the quantity of links.

For local businesses, local relevance of links matters as much as authority.

## Priority Link Sources for Local Business

**High value (pursue first):**
1. Local newspaper/news site — Twin Falls Times-News, Magic Valley news sites
2. Local business associations — Twin Falls Area Chamber of Commerce
3. Industry directories — RepairPal, AutoMD, Carfax Service Shop Finder
4. Local event sponsorships — sponsor a community event, get a link from the event page
5. Supplier/vendor sites — if you're an authorized dealer or certified shop
6. Local bloggers/YouTubers who cover Twin Falls

**Medium value:**
- Yelp, Yellow Pages, BBB — lower authority but expected for local businesses
- Industry publications — automotive magazines, trade publications
- Guest posts on automotive blogs

## What to Avoid

- Paid link schemes (violates Google guidelines, risk penalty)
- Link farms and private blog networks
- Low-quality directory submissions in bulk
- Reciprocal link exchanges ("link to me, I'll link to you")
- Footer/sidebar links across many sites

## Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links are controllable and underutilized. They:
- Pass authority from high-traffic pages to important landing pages
- Help Google discover all pages
- Increase time on site by surfacing relevant content

Pattern for jrs-auto-repair:
```
Homepage → Service pages (from services section)
Service pages → Related how-to articles ("Learn more about why brakes wear out")
Articles → Related service pages ("Need brake repair? We're in Twin Falls...")
Articles → Related articles (2-3 relevant internal links per article)
```

## Anchor Text Guidelines

Anchor text is the clickable text of a link. Vary it naturally:

```typescript
// In article body — vary anchor text
// Good variation:
<Link href="/services/brake-repair">brake repair in Twin Falls</Link>
<Link href="/services/brake-repair">our Twin Falls brake specialists</Link>
<Link href="/services/brake-repair">brake inspection service</Link>

// Avoid: always using exact match keyword as anchor text
// Avoid: "click here" as anchor text — no keyword value
```

## Content as Link Bait

"Link bait" = content people want to link to. For a local auto repair shop:

- **Local statistics** — "Average annual car repair costs in Idaho" (aggregated data)
- **Original research** — survey of Twin Falls drivers on maintenance habits
- **Comprehensive guides** — "Complete Guide to Car Maintenance for Idaho Weather"
- **Free tools** — maintenance calculator, cost estimator
- **Local expert opinion** — news sites sometimes quote local business owners

## Tracking Links in SEO Tools

Use Google Search Console to see who links to the site:
- Search Console → Links → Top linking sites

Also Ahrefs/Moz for competitive analysis — see who links to competitors, then pursue those same sources.

## Earning Links Through Content

The most sustainable link building strategy is publishing content that naturally earns citations:

1. **Authoritative service guides** — detailed enough that other sites reference them
2. **Local resource pages** — "Best auto repair shops in Twin Falls" (the page you want to be linked FROM)
3. **Community involvement** — sponsor, volunteer, partner → local organizations link to partners

The pattern: create content → email relevant sites/journalists about it → some link without asking.

## Link Building vs Citation Building

These are different:
- **Link** = HTML `<a href>` pointing to your domain — affects organic rankings
- **Citation** = NAP mention anywhere (even without a link) — affects local/maps rankings

Both matter for local SEO. Prioritize Google Business Profile citations first, then pursue traditional links.
